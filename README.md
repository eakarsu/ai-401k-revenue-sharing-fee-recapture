# 401(k) Revenue-Sharing & Fee Recapture

Calculate revenue sharing and required fee credits to prevent excess recordkeeping compensation and participant inequity.

**Primary buyer:** Plan sponsors, advisers, and fiduciaries. **Evidence:** recordkeeping agreements, investment options, participant balances, revenue-sharing rates, sub-TA fees, expense ratios, fee credits, plan expenses, and allocations.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Recordkeeping agreement library
- Investment option registry
- Participant balance ingestion
- Revenue-sharing rate mapping
- 12b-1 calculation
- Sub-TA fee calculation
- Shareholder service fee
- Gross-to-net expense review
- Required revenue credit
- Recordkeeper statement audit
- Participant allocation method
- Excess fee detection
- Credit correction workflow
- Fiduciary evidence package
- Plan option analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4677>. API: `5677`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
